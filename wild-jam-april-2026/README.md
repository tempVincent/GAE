# ☕ Coffee Break

> A cozy pixel-art delivery game made in one week for **Godot Wild Jam #92**.

🎮 **[Play it on itch.io](https://tempvinc.itch.io/coffee-break)**

---

## 📖 About

You play as **Silvester**, a hardworking employee stuck in a dull office job. To escape the monotony, you dedicate your breaks to something meaningful: brewing the perfect drinks for your coworker **Jonny**.

He'll always tell you exactly what he wants — your job is to deliver it.

Made by **tempVinc**, **Benekov** and **Fohlii** — our very first published game, built with only a month of Godot experience. We're incredibly proud of what we achieved!

---

## 🛠️ Interactions

| Object          | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **Coffee Can**  | Your all-purpose tool — carry coffee, water, or juice to Jonny |
| **Coffee Machine** | Interact with it (while holding the can) to fill it with fresh coffee |
| **Tap**         | Fill your can with water — note: water isn't needed before making coffee! |
| **Mixer**       | Turn bananas into juice, then fill your can with the result |
| **Bananas**     | Catch falling bananas and bring them to the mixer |
| **Delivery Table** | Place the can (or a banana) in front of Jonny to complete an order |
